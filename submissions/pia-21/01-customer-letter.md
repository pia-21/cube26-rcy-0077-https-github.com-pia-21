# Customer Letter — Recovery Manager

Dear Seller / Ecommerce Operator,

Fees, reimbursements, and other financial adjustments can appear after the operational event that caused them. By the time a charge is reviewed, the evidence needed to understand or challenge it may be spread across receiving, preparation, packing, and returns records.

Recovery Manager helps investigate these charges systematically.

It takes a fee or reimbursement report, identifies the associated unit and order information, retrieves relevant operational evidence, and compares the documented evidence with the basis of the charge.

For each charge, it produces a structured result:

- **SUPPORTED** — the available evidence supports the documented basis of the charge.
- **CONTRADICTED** — the available evidence directly conflicts with the charge.
- **SILENT** — relevant evidence is not available to evaluate the charge.
- **UNCERTAIN** — the available information is ambiguous or the AI evaluation cannot safely reach a conclusion.

When a charge is contradicted and the evidence supports a recovery claim, Recovery Manager records the potential claim amount and the evidence used to support it.

The system is deliberately conservative. Missing evidence is not treated as proof that a charge is wrong, and the agent does not invent evidence or claim amounts. When it cannot establish a defensible conclusion, it leaves the case for review instead.

The goal is not to maximize the number of claims.

The goal is to produce claims that can be defended with documented operational evidence.

Sincerely,

Recovery Manager
