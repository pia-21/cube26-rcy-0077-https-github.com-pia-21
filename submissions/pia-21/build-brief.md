# Recovery Manager — Build Brief

## Problem
Companies receive fees/charges and may not have the evidence easily available to dispute them.
## Goal
Find evidence for each charge and decide whether the charge is justified.
## Input
- Fee/reimburstment records 
- Receiving records 
- Prep records 
- Pack records
- Return records
## Core Flow
Charge → Identify unit/order/shipment → Find relevant evidence → Compare → Verdict → Claim + explanation
## Kill Condition
If we cannot reliably connect a charge to relevant evidence, we do not make a recovery claim.
